Project overview

A clearer view of patient health risks

The project analyzes age, BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, smoking, alcohol consumption, fatigue, chest pain, dizziness and exercise levels. Tableau views help compare groups, explore patterns and communicate findings.

Analysis perspectives

Clinical risk assessment

Compare clinical indicators such as blood pressure, BMI, cholesterol and glucose to explore potential high-risk patient segments.

Lifestyle patterns

Explore how smoking, alcohol consumption and exercise levels vary across patient groups and how they relate to reported outcomes.

Population health

Compare age groups and BMI categories to identify segments that may warrant closer public-health attention.

What the analysis includes

Nine Tableau visualizations

These are the nine views listed in the project description. The embedded Tableau dashboard below is the interactive source for the published visualizations.

1. KPI — Patient Health & Disease Overview

High-level patient and disease metrics for a quick overview of the dataset.

2. Bar Chart — Average Blood Pressure Across Age Groups

Compare average blood pressure by age category. The supplied project notes report the Old group at 135.67 mmHg.

3. Grouped Bar — Alcohol and Smoking by Age

Compare reported smoking and alcohol consumption patterns across age groups.

4. Donut Chart — Exercise Level Distribution

Show the distribution of exercise levels across the patient population.

5. Pie Chart — Patient Distribution by Age Group

Summarize how the dataset's patient records are distributed among age groups.

6. Grouped Bar — Heart Disease vs Diabetes by BMI

Compare heart disease and diabetes prevalence across BMI categories.

7. Bar Chart — BMI Category vs Heart Disease Rate

Compare the recorded heart disease rate between BMI categories.

8. Heat Map — Heart Disease by BMI and Exercise

Use color intensity to explore heart disease patterns across BMI categories and exercise levels.

9. Bar Chart — Age Group by Fatigue Condition

Compare fatigue-condition distribution across the defined age groups.
