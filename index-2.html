Project overview

Turning healthcare data into clearer insights

Health360 uses interactive visual analytics to compare patient groups and explore patterns across health and lifestyle variables.

Patient demographics

Review patient distribution across age groups and compare health indicators between demographic segments.

Disease prevalence

Compare heart disease, diabetes, and stroke indicators, including how heart disease rates vary by BMI category.

Lifestyle risk factors

Explore exercise levels, alcohol and smoking consumption, and fatigue conditions alongside other patient attributes.

What to explore

Key analysis areas

Use these themes as a guide while interacting with the dashboard and story.

Blood pressure across age groups

Compare average blood pressure values across the age categories represented in the dataset.

Alcohol and smoking

Compare the recorded alcohol and smoking measures across age groups where available in the workbook.

BMI and disease prevalence

Review heart disease and diabetes measures by BMI category, and examine heart disease rates across categories.

Exercise and fatigue

Explore exercise-level distribution, the heat map of heart disease by BMI and exercise level, and fatigue across age groups.

Methodology

How to use this project

The site presents Tableau Public visualizations alongside context to help visitors navigate the analysis.

01

Start with the overview

Open the dashboard and review the overall indicators and categories included in the published workbook.

02

Filter and compare

Use available Tableau filters and chart interactions to compare patient groups and risk-factor measures.

03

Follow the story

Continue to the story section for the accompanying visual narrative and use the Tableau Public link if needed.

Interpretation note: This project is for educational and exploratory data-analysis purposes. Observed patterns in the dataset do not establish causation and should not be used as individual medical advice or diagnosis.
